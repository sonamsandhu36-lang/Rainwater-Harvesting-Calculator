HOW IT WORKS

The Science Behind Rainwater Harvesting

Rainwater Harvesting Formula

Water Yield (L) = Area × Rainfall × Runoff Coefficient × Efficiency

One millimetre of rainfall over one square metre of area equals approximately one litre of water.
